Privacy Policy

Daylight Room Co respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you contact us, request or receive our interior design services, or visit our website, where applicable. We process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection laws, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information we collect may include your name, contact details, project address, information about your design requirements, communications with us, and details needed to prepare quotes, manage projects and process payments. If you visit our website, we may also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and information about how you use the site. We may receive information from third parties where necessary to deliver a project, such as contractors or suppliers, and will handle it in accordance with this policy.

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes and interior design services, communicate with you, manage projects and payments, maintain business records, improve our services and website, and meet our legal obligations. We rely on appropriate legal bases for processing, which may include taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract, complying with legal obligations, pursuing our legitimate business interests, or your consent where required.

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us run our business or deliver projects, such as IT, payment, accounting, delivery and project-related providers. We require them to protect your information and use it only for the agreed purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights. We do not sell your personal information.

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, meet legal and accounting requirements, and resolve disputes. The appropriate retention period depends on the type of information and why it was collected.

If our website uses cookies or similar technologies, these may be used to operate the site, remember preferences or understand site usage. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Your information is stored securely, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure. Where information is transferred outside the UK, we will use appropriate safeguards as required by data protection law.

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, or receive a copy in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are concerned about how your information is handled.

To ask a question or exercise your rights, contact Daylight Room Co using the contact details provided in our communications or on our website, if available. We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on our website, where applicable.
